# "War Horn · Borderline" Rulebook v6.2

**Type**: Two-player turn-based strategy board game
**Duration**: 30–60 minutes per game (online)

## Part I: Core Rules

### Chapter 1: The Board & Forces

#### 1.1 The Board

- 19×19 board (361 intersection points).
- **Row 10 = the Borderline**, belonging to neither side.
- **Rows 1–9 = Black's territory**; its boundaries include the top edge and the row 1–9 segments of the left and right edges.
- **Rows 11–19 = White's territory**; its boundaries include the bottom edge and the row 11–19 segments of the left and right edges.

#### 1.2 Forces

- Each side has a force limit (default 120 stones, configurable), non-renewable.
- A side that runs out of stones cannot place any more; when neither side can place a stone, the game ends immediately.

### Chapter 2: Scoring Overview

**Total Score = Occupation + Defense − Casualties**

All scores are computed live and dynamically during play.

| Score Type | Components | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Occupation** | Live stones + Territory | Control exerted in the opponent's territory |
| **Defense** | Annihilation + Siege | Defensive results in your own territory |
| **Casualties** | — | The cost of being captured |

#### 2.1 Komi

**White receives +0.5 points of komi compensation.**

- At endgame settlement, White's total is increased by 0.5.
- Since all scores are integers, this is equivalent to: **when the totals are equal, White wins**.
- Komi only affects the endgame win/loss decision; it does not change the in-game score display.

### Chapter 3: Occupation Score

**Occupation = Live Stones + Territory**

#### 3.1 Live Stones

**Definition**: A stone placed in the **opponent's territory** or on the **Borderline** immediately earns +1 live-stone point if it has liberties after placement. No upper limit.

| Trigger | Change |
|---|---|
| Placed in the **opponent's territory** or on the **Borderline**, with liberties after placement | +1/stone |
| Captured (under any circumstances) | −1/stone |
| Judged as sieged | −1/stone |
| Siege lifted (making eyes / breaking out / empty points restored) | +1/stone |

**Has liberties**: the connected group containing the stone has at least one adjacent empty intersection.

#### 3.2 Territory Score

The full territory rules are described in **Chapter 4: Enclosures and Territory**.

### Chapter 4: Enclosures and Territory

#### 4.1 Definition and Judgment of an Enclosure

**Enclosure**: a fully closed geometric boundary formed on the board by one side's stones.

**Components**:
- **Enclosing stones**: your own stones that connect directly or indirectly through up/down/left/right adjacency, forming a continuous wall.
- **Board edges**: the four edges act as natural walls and may participate in an enclosure, but the **two endpoints** of the participating edge segments must be occupied by stones.
- **Closure**: the boundary connects end to end with no gap.

**Judgment**: purely geometric. Starting from any point outside the enclosure, one must pass through the enclosing side's stone chain or a board edge to reach the interior.

**Impure boundary stones**: a closed region jointly formed by both sides' stones is not recognized as an enclosure for either side.

**Valid enclosure**: an enclosure formed by **live** stones. Enclosures formed by sieged stones are invalid.

#### 4.2 Territory Score Calculation

**Basic calculation**: forming a valid enclosure in the opponent's territory or on the Borderline earns **+2 points per enclosed intersection**.

**Deduct the opponent's live stones**: intersections occupied by the opponent's live stones inside the enclosure do not count toward the territory score.

**Opponent's sieged stones**: intersections occupied by the opponent's sieged stones inside the enclosure DO count toward the enclosing side's territory score (captured).

**Own stones**: intersections occupied by your own stones inside the enclosure (whether alive or sieged) do not affect the territory calculation.

**Effective territory points = total intersections inside the enclosure − intersections occupied by the opponent's live stones**

**Dynamic update**: when an enclosure forms / expands / shrinks / is broken through, the score changes in real time.

#### 4.3 Nested Enclosures

Judged layer by layer from the inside out:
- The inner layer is formed by **live** stones → the inner region belongs to the inner side, and the outer enclosure's effective territory points are reduced by the entire inner region.
- The inner layer is formed by **sieged** stones → the inner layer is invalid and the region belongs to the outer valid enclosure.

Dynamic update: when the life/death status of inner-layer stones changes, the nested ownership is re-judged immediately and both sides' territory scores are updated.

#### 4.4 Score Changes on Status Transitions of Enclosed Stones

**I. Status transitions of the attacker's stones (attacker stones inside the defender's enclosure)**

| Transition | Trigger | Defender's Siege | Attacker's Live | Territory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alive → Sieged | validly enclosed + no two eyes + empty points < 4 | +1/stone | −1/stone | the point starts counting toward the defender's territory |
| Sieged → Alive (making eyes) | two eyes made | −1/stone | +1/stone | the point no longer counts toward the defender's territory |
| Sieged → Captured | captured | −1/stone | — | Annihilation +2; Casualty −1 |
| Sieged → Broken out | enclosure broken | −1/stone | +1/stone | the defender's enclosure disappears; all territory deducted |
| Sieged → Empty points restored | empty points back to ≥ 4 | −1/stone | +1/stone | the point no longer counts toward the defender's territory |

**II. Status transitions of the defender's enclosing stones**

| Transition | Trigger | Attacker's Siege | Defender's Live | Territory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alive → Sieged | validly counter-enclosed + siege conditions met | +1/stone (if applicable) | −1/stone (if applicable) | the defender's territory for that enclosure is deducted |
| Sieged → Alive | siege lifted | −1/stone | +1/stone (if applicable) | the defender's territory for that enclosure is restored |

**Nested update**: when the status of the defender's enclosing stones changes, ownership of the regions inside that enclosure is re-judged immediately.

#### 4.5 Borderline Special Rules

The Borderline (row 10) belongs to neither side. Both sides may earn Occupation and Defense points there. For enclosures on the Borderline: empty points inside earn +2 Occupation, and the opponent's sieged stones inside earn +1 Defense; both may stack.

### Chapter 5: Defense Score

**Defense = Annihilation + Siege**

#### 5.1 Annihilation

| Trigger | Change |
|---|---|
| Capturing opponent stones in **your own territory** | +2/stone |
| Capturing opponent stones on the **Borderline** | +2/stone |
| Capturing opponent stones in the **opponent's territory** | **No annihilation** |

**Note**: Annihilation measures defensive results and only counts when you eliminate enemies in your own territory or on the Borderline. The value of captures in the opponent's territory is already reflected through the territory score.

**When capturing a sieged stone**: first lift the siege (Siege −1), then gain Annihilation +2. Net defense change +1. The attacker's Casualty still applies −1.

#### 5.2 Siege

**Siege definition**: surrounded by the opponent's **valid enclosure** + has not made two true eyes + legal empty points inside the enclosure < 4.

**Siege settlement** (live and dynamic):

| Event | Siege change | Live-stone change | Territory change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entering siege** | Defender +1/stone | Attacker −1/stone | the stone's point starts counting toward the defender's territory |
| **Siege lifted** | Defender −1/stone | Attacker +1/stone | the stone's point no longer counts toward the defender's territory |

**Siege regional rules**:

| Enclosure location | Siege points |
|---|---|
| Inside your own territory | +1/stone |
| On the Borderline | +1/stone (stacks with territory) |
| Inside the opponent's territory | none (covered by territory) |

**Ways to lift a siege**:
1. Making life (making two true eyes)
2. Breaking through the enclosure (the enclosure is broken)
3. Empty points restored to ≥ 4
4. Being captured (Siege −1, Annihilation +2, Casualty −1)

### Chapter 6: Siege Judgment

#### 6.1 When to Judge

Whenever the board state changes (a stone placed or captured), the system automatically checks whether the relevant stones satisfy the siege conditions. The result is immediately reflected in the scores.

#### 6.2 Basic Principle

**Stones have only two states: live and sieged.**

#### 6.3 Live Judgment (alive if ANY condition holds)

1. **Has two true eyes**: regardless of being surrounded or the number of empty points.
2. **Not surrounded by a valid enclosure of the opponent**.
3. **Surrounded by a valid enclosure, but legal placement points inside ≥ 4**.

#### 6.4 Siege Judgment (ALL three conditions must hold)

1. **Surrounded by a valid enclosure of the opponent**.
2. **Does not have two true eyes**.
3. **Legal placement points inside < 4**.

**Valid enclosure**: a closed geometric boundary formed by **live** stones. Enclosures formed by sieged stones are invalid.

#### 6.5 Independent True Eye Judgment

An eye point is an independent true eye if and only if the opponent placing a stone there would be suicide (a forbidden point).

#### 6.6 Legal Empty Points

Simulated placement: if a stone placed at the point has liberties, or — although without liberties — immediately captures opponent stones, the point is a legal placement; otherwise it is a forbidden point and does not count as an empty point.

#### 6.7 Judgment Quick Reference

| Has two true eyes? | Validly surrounded? | Empty points < 4? | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yes | — | — | **Live** |
| No | No | — | **Live** |
| No | Yes | No | **Live** |
| No | Yes | Yes | **Sieged** |

#### 6.8 Important Notes

- There are no "dead stones"; only live and sieged.
- There is no "seki (mutual life)"; when two sides surround each other, each side is judged independently.
- On-screen indicator: sieged stones always show a chain icon during play.

### Chapter 7: Casualty Score

| Trigger | Change |
|---|---|
| Actually captured by the opponent at **any location** | −1/stone |

**Casualty and Siege**: when a stone is captured, if it was sieged at that moment, first execute the siege lift (Siege −1), then the Casualty −1, and finally the Annihilation +2 (if applicable).

### Chapter 8: Ko

#### 8.1 Move Rules

Repetition of the whole-board position is forbidden. After capturing in a ko, the opponent must find a ko threat and may not immediately recapture.

#### 8.2 In-Game Score Settlement

Every capture is settled as usual: capturing in your own territory / on the Borderline → Annihilation +2; the captured side → Casualty −1; also check whether the siege status changed.

#### 8.3 Endgame Handling

At the end of the game the ko is not played out. The ownership is decided by "who benefits more from the ko":
- Simulate both sides winning separately and compute each side's net score increase (including changes to Annihilation, Casualty, Live stones, Territory, and Siege).
- Compare the net increases: the larger side wins the ko; if equal, it is awarded to the side that actually controls the ko stone at the end of the game.

### Chapter 9: Deployment Phase

#### 9.1 Definition

After the game starts, the first 2 rounds (4 moves total) form the **deployment phase**.

- Black's 1st move: must be placed in **Black's territory** (rows 1–9).
- White's 1st move: must be placed in **White's territory** (rows 11–19).
- Black's 2nd move: must be placed in **Black's territory** (rows 1–9).
- White's 2nd move: must be placed in **White's territory** (rows 11–19).

After the deployment phase, from **move 5** (Black) onward the formal combat begins; stones may be placed on any legal intersection.

#### 9.2 Deployment Rules

- **Placement area**: only your own territory; the Borderline and the opponent's territory are not allowed.
- **Scoring**: placing in your own territory during deployment earns no live-stone, territory, annihilation, or siege points.
- **Captures**: during deployment both sides' stones are inside their own territories, so captures are impossible.
- **Passing forbidden**: during deployment you must place a stone; passing is not allowed.
- **Force consumption**: deployment placements consume forces normally.
- **Enclosures**: enclosures formed in your own territory during deployment do not generate territory points.
- **Timing**: each side has its own independent clock (default 2 minutes each); only the side whose turn it is has its time deducted. When a side runs out of time, the system automatically places random stones for it inside its own territory until it has placed 2 stones and completes its deployment.

#### 9.3 Deployment Strategy Notes

| Placement | Strategic intent |
|---|---|
| **Near the Borderline** | Prepare to fight for the line from move 5 onward |
| **A corner of your own territory** | Lay the initial foundation for later enclosures |
| **Near the center** | Establish a flexible initial foothold |

### Chapter 10: Moves and Endgame

#### 10.1 Moves

- Black moves first.
- **Deployment phase** (first 4 moves): must be placed in your own territory.
- **Combat phase** (from move 5): may be placed on any legal intersection.
- Players alternate; stones without liberties are captured (settling Annihilation / Casualty); suicide is forbidden.
- **Ko**: repetition of the whole-board position is forbidden.
- Passing is allowed (except during deployment), with a **pass cooldown of 2 of your own turns**: after a side passes, it must complete 2 of its own turns (place / deploy / bounce) before it may pass again; a side that has never passed may pass at any time. Two consecutive passes by both sides → endgame.
- **After every move the system checks automatically**: enclosure formation/breaking → territory updated; siege status changes → siege and live-stone points updated.

#### 10.2 Forced Endgame

One side runs out of stones and both sides pass consecutively → endgame.

#### 10.3 Endgame Settlement

1. Both sides have passed consecutively.
2. The system handles the ko (simulated judgment).
3. A final life/death judgment is performed on the current board and all dynamic scores are frozen.
4. Read both sides' final totals. **White's total gets an additional +0.5 komi**.
5. Compare: **Black's total vs White's total + 0.5**. The higher side wins. Equal scores mean a draw.

## Part II: Optional Rules

### Optional Rule: Special Forces (Covert Operations)

#### Rule Attributes

- **Type**: optional add-on rule.
- **Applicable modes**: custom rooms, total war mode.
- **Default state**: off.

#### Skill Details

| Attribute | Setting |
|---|---|
| Uses | 2 per game, cannot be used consecutively |
| Duration | 20 turns (20 moves by each side), automatically revealed when expired |
| Stone consumption | does not count toward the force limit |

**Announcement**: when activated, a public announcement is shown to all; the specific position is not revealed.

**Deployment scoring**: no immediate score. Its value lies in the endgame rewards.

**Casualty**: when captured, the casualty is **−6 points** (the opponent still earns Annihilation +2 as usual).

**Success rewards** (three mutually exclusive; at endgame the best one applies):

1. **Territory involvement**: the stone is alive in the opponent's territory and participates in an enclosure → that enclosure's territory score is **doubled**.
2. **Covert survival**: the stone is alive in the opponent's territory but does not participate in an enclosure → the stone counts as **+3 Occupation** (replacing the normal +1).
3. **Borderline contribution**: the stone is alive on the Borderline and participates in an enclosure → the **total Defense score produced by that enclosure increases by 50%** (rounded up).

**Exposure conditions**: the opponent lands on that point or one of its four adjacent cells, or the 20 turns expire.

**Overlap handling**: when the opponent's placement overlaps a hidden stone, the hidden stone is revealed and the opponent's placement is randomly bounced to one of the 8 surrounding cells.

**Endgame handling**: all unrevealed hidden stones are revealed and settled by the normal rules, and it is judged whether success rewards trigger.
